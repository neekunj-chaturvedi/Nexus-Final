# Template: Escalation Acknowledgement

Use when a request has been escalated to a human reviewer, whether by
confidence-based or hard category-based escalation. Never state the
internal escalation category name to the customer; use the neutral
framing below.

---

Subject: We're looking into your request, {{customer_name}}

Hello {{customer_name}},

Thank you for reaching out. Your request has been passed to a member of
our team for personal review, and you don't need to do anything further
right now. We'll follow up directly, usually within one business day.

If anything about your situation changes in the meantime, please let us
know.

Meridian Financial Group
