# Template: KYC Re-Verification Notice

Use when a request cannot be completed because the customer's KYC status
is `pending` or `expired`, and the underlying account/portfolio shows as
restricted or frozen as a result.

---

Hello {{customer_name}},

We're not able to complete this request yet because your KYC details need
to be re-verified. This is also why you may see your {{affected_product}}
showing as {{affected_status}}.

To resolve this, please complete re-verification at your nearest branch or
through the Meridian app. Once that's done, the restriction is lifted
automatically — there's no separate request needed.

Meridian Financial Group
