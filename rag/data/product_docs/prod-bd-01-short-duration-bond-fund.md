# Meridian Short Duration Bond Fund (PROD-BD-01) — Fund Fact Sheet

**Asset class:** Debt
**Risk level:** Low
**Suitable risk profiles:** Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive
**Minimum investment:** INR 1,000
**Expense ratio:** 0.45%

## Investment Objective

The fund invests in short-duration government securities and AAA-rated
corporate bonds, aiming to provide stable income with low sensitivity to
interest-rate movements relative to longer-duration debt funds.

## Who This Fund Is For

Because of its low risk level, this fund is broadly suitable across all
three risk profiles Meridian recognises — `conservative`, `moderate`, and
`aggressive` — making it a common building block in diversified
portfolios such as **PORT-20077**, which already holds this fund
alongside a hybrid allocation.

## Key Risks

- Interest-rate risk: bond prices move inversely to rates, though the
  short duration limits the size of the swing.
- Credit risk is minimal given the AAA/government-only mandate, but is
  not zero.

## Fees

The 0.45% expense ratio is among the lowest in Meridian's fund range,
reflecting the lower management complexity of short-duration debt.
