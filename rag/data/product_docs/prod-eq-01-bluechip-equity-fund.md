# Meridian Bluechip Equity Fund (PROD-EQ-01) — Fund Fact Sheet

**Asset class:** Equity
**Risk level:** High
**Suitable risk profiles:** Aggressive
**Minimum investment:** INR 5,000
**Expense ratio:** 1.20%

## Investment Objective

The fund seeks long-term capital appreciation by investing primarily in
large-capitalisation Indian equities across established sectors. It is
designed for investors with a long holding horizon who can tolerate
significant short-term price swings in exchange for higher expected
long-term returns.

## Who This Fund Is For

This fund is suitable only for customers whose risk profile is assessed as
`aggressive`. It is not suitable for `conservative` or `moderate` risk
profiles — recommending this fund outside an aggressive profile should
fail the suitability check (`wealth_server.check_product_suitability`)
rather than being approved on the strength of this description alone.

## Key Risks

- Equity markets can and do decline sharply over short periods.
- Concentration in large-cap names reduces, but does not eliminate,
  volatility relative to broader equity exposure.
- Past performance of the underlying holdings does not guarantee future
  results; this document does not cite historical returns and none
  should be inferred from it.

## Fees

The 1.20% annual expense ratio covers fund management and administration
and is accrued daily into the fund's NAV; it is not billed separately to
the investor.
