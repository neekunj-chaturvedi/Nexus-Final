# Meridian Gold Savings Fund (PROD-GD-01) — Fund Fact Sheet

**Asset class:** Commodity (Gold-linked fund of funds)
**Risk level:** Medium
**Suitable risk profiles:** Moderate, Aggressive
**Minimum investment:** INR 500
**Expense ratio:** 0.60%

## Investment Objective

The fund invests in units of underlying gold exchange-traded funds,
offering indirect gold exposure without the need to hold physical gold or
a demat-linked ETF position directly.

## Who This Fund Is For

Suitable for `moderate` and `aggressive` risk profiles seeking a
diversifying, non-equity, non-debt asset. Not typically recommended as a
primary holding — gold is better suited as a smaller diversifying sleeve
within a broader portfolio than as a core position.

## Key Risks

- Gold prices can be volatile over short periods and are driven by global
  macro factors largely unrelated to Indian equity or debt markets.
- A fund of funds structure layers the underlying ETF's expense ratio on
  top of this fund's own, which is already reflected in the 0.60% figure
  above.

## Fees

The 0.60% expense ratio is inclusive of underlying fund costs.
