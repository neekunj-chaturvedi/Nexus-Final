# Meridian Balanced Advantage Fund (PROD-HY-01) — Fund Fact Sheet

**Asset class:** Hybrid
**Risk level:** Medium
**Suitable risk profiles:** Moderate, Aggressive
**Minimum investment:** INR 2,500
**Expense ratio:** 0.95%

## Investment Objective

The fund dynamically allocates between equity and debt based on prevailing
market valuations, aiming to capture equity upside while cushioning
drawdowns through tactical debt exposure.

## Who This Fund Is For

Suitable for `moderate` and `aggressive` risk profiles. It is **not**
suitable for a `conservative` risk profile — the equity component can
still produce meaningful drawdowns that a conservative investor should not
be exposed to. This fund forms the largest holding in portfolio
**PORT-20077**, consistent with that customer's `moderate` risk profile.

## Key Risks

- Allocation shifts are tactical, not guaranteed to reduce losses in every
  downturn.
- Blends equity volatility and debt interest-rate risk, though typically
  at a lower combined intensity than a pure equity fund.

## Fees

The 0.95% expense ratio reflects the active, dynamically-managed
allocation strategy.
