# Meridian Travel Insurance — Policy Wording (Product Class: travel)

## 1. Scope of Cover

Applies to all Meridian Travel Insurance policies (policy numbers prefixed
`TRP`), including policy **POL-TR-30094**.

## 2. Covered Scenarios

- `trip_cancellation` — non-refundable costs from a covered cancellation
  reason
- `lost_baggage` — checked baggage lost by a common carrier

## 3. Exclusions

- `pre_existing_condition` — any claim connected to a pre-existing medical
  condition disclosed, or that should have been disclosed, at purchase

## 4. Premium Payment Terms

Travel policies are annual and, unlike the home and motor product classes,
are paid by `card` only — auto-debit is not offered on this product class.
A customer asking to set up auto-debit for a travel premium must be told
this is not available for this product, rather than being routed through
the same auto-debit flow used for home or motor policies.

## 5. Claims

Claims are tracked via `insurance_server.get_claim_status`.
